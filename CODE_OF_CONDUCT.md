# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the `symaira-scope` project a
welcoming, respectful, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
  project spaces.
- Communicate clearly and assume good faith while discussing technical
  disagreements.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact, not on the person who contributed
  it.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, correct them when possible, and help keep
  project spaces useful and accessible.
- Protect other people's privacy and avoid sharing information that was not
  intended to be public.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
threats, personal attacks, sustained disruption, sexualized language or
attention, unwanted contact, publishing private information, doxxing,
impersonation, and any other conduct that would reasonably make participation
unsafe or hostile. Deliberate abuse of the `symscope` project, its services, or
its contributors is also not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, events, and other spaces where a person represents the
`symaira-scope` project. It also applies when conduct outside those spaces has a
material effect on a person's safety or ability to participate in the project.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may remove or edit comments, close discussions, decline
contributions, or take other action they consider appropriate, including a
warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect the privacy of people involved
as far as practical, and communicate the outcome to the reporter when it is
appropriate to do so. They will not disclose private report details beyond
those who need them to handle the report.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness conduct that violates this Code of Conduct, report
it privately to **security@danieljustus.dev**. Please include the relevant
project space, links or dates if available, and a description of what happened.
Do not use a public issue for a conduct report.

This address is the confidential contact for Code of Conduct reports; it is not
the project's private vulnerability-reporting channel. To report a security
vulnerability, do not open a public issue or send sensitive vulnerability
details as a conduct report. Instead, use GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting link in [the security policy](.github/SECURITY.md).
